Getting started

StudyLab keeps homework, tests, projects, flashcards and focus sessions in one place. Add schoolwork from the Plan area, then use Home and Focus to decide what to work on.

StudyLab Pro

StudyLab Pro is a one-time lifetime purchase. It unlocks advanced tools such as revision planning, A/B schedules, iCloud backup, unlimited planning and premium themes.

Restore Purchases

If you already bought StudyLab Pro, open StudyLab Pro and choose Restore Purchases. Make sure the device is signed into the same Apple Account that originally made the purchase.

iCloud backup

StudyLab stores its main database on your device. StudyLab Pro can create an explicit compact backup in your private iCloud account and restore that backup later.

Notifications

Deadline and focus alerts use Apple’s local notification system. You can change notification permission at any time in iOS Settings.

Privacy

StudyLab does not include advertising or third-party analytics in version 1.0. Read the full policy for details about local storage, iCloud and purchases.
